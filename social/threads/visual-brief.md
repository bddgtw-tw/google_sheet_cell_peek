# Visual brief

## Best first asset

Use one 5–10 second GIF or short screen recording:

1. Show a Google Sheets cell with long text.
2. Click the cell and show the blue selection frame.
3. Show the floating preview window appearing after about 0.3 seconds.
4. Move the window slightly.
5. Click Copy or Edit.

## Screenshot requirements

- Hide or crop private spreadsheet data.
- Keep the floating window large enough to read.
- Show the blue cell selection frame.
- Avoid showing account names, document IDs, or unrelated browser tabs.
- Add a small English label: `Long cell text, without expanding the sheet`.
